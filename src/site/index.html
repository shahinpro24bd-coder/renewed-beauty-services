About Dr. Fatema Mirza

Who is Dr. Fatema Mirza?

Dr. Fatema Mirza is a plastic and aesthetic surgeon devoted to restoring form, function and confidence — from complex reconstructive surgery to refined aesthetic procedures.

Her practice has a special focus on neurofibroma and plexiform neurofibroma management, along with skin and soft-tissue reconstruction. Every consultation begins with listening, and every treatment plan is built around the person in front of her — never a template.
